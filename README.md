## 🎮 What's in the pipeline?

### Meteorfrogs: *main project*
A premium grid puzzle game for PC, in Unity. You control vents across a 13×11 crater, pushing rows of mutated frogs until matching species touch and explode. It's a spiritual successor to *Rings of the Magi*.

- Five species, told apart by **shape and animation, not colour alone**
- A custom plain-text level format (LevelText) and a single-file HTML level editor
- An optional story layer with a guide character who hints at ideas, never moves

### Akali's Colouring Day
A drawing and colouring app for young kids. **Free, no ads, no subscriptions**: just an optional one-off "thank you" behind a parental gate. I built it because so many kids' art apps hide a yearly subscription behind a "free" download. Web/PWA first, now in Unity with proper stylus support.

### Side builds
- **Speak Like A Human**: a typing game where you translate corporate jargon into plain English (web + Unity)
- **Math Adventure**: A simple gamified math quiz with content from Grades 1-7, built alongside my son (web)
- Small utilities along the way: a remote-job aggregator (FastAPI + SQLite) and a styled QR code generator

## 🧠 How I work

- **Accessibility is a hard constraint, not a feature.** I'm red-green colour-blind, so nothing I make relies on colour alone.
- **Fair monetization.** Pay once, or pay nothing. No dark patterns, no corporate BS tactics, especially in anything for kids.
- **Data habits carry over.** Plain-text formats, validation and tooling come first, so content is easy to build and test.
- **Ship small, then grow it.** Prototype in the browser, then commit to the engine.
